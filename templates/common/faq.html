Getting registered takes less than two minutes:

  1. Click on Register in the top navigation bar.
  2. Select your role: Student, Alumni, or Faculty.
  3. Provide your name, official email, phone number, and academic enrollment/graduation details.
  4. Verify your email with the 6-digit OTP sent to your inbox.
  5. Log in and start connecting!
Alumni accounts undergo quick verification against university records within 24 hours.

Account recovery is fully automated and secure:

  1. Navigate to the Forgot Password page.
  2. Enter your registered email address.
  3. A single-use 6-digit OTP will be dispatched immediately (valid for 10 minutes).
  4. Enter the OTP, set your new strong password, and access is restored right away!
