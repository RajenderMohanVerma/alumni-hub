Information Sharing & Zero-Sale Guarantee

Strict Policy: We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third-party ad brokers or marketing agencies.

Your profile is visible only to verified alumni, current students, and faculty administrators of the institution. We share data only when required by applicable law, accredited university audits, or essential cloud infrastructure providers (e.g. secure email dispatching and database hosting).

SECTION 5

Security Architecture & Safeguards

Strong Password Hashing

Passwords are salted and hashed using modern bcrypt/PBKDF2 algorithms. Plain text passwords are never stored anywhere in our database.

TLS 1.3 Transmission

All network traffic between your browser and our servers is encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security (TLS).

Single-Use Ephemeral OTPs

Verification and password reset codes expire strictly in 10 minutes and are purged from database records upon single use.

Role-Based Access Control

Strict permission boundaries ensure students, alumni, and administrators can only access authorized endpoints.
